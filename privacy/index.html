Privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026

This policy explains how Valorwright Games LLC handles information when you use our website, valorwright.com, and our mobile game DNA Forge. DNA Forge on Android uses the package name com.valorwright.dnaforge. The same policy covers the Apple version of the game. It is written in plain language.

Who we are

Valorwright Games LLC is a Texas limited liability company. In this policy, “we” and “the studio” mean Valorwright Games LLC. This policy covers the website and DNA Forge.

Privacy questions go to contact@valorwright.com.

The website

You can read the site without an account. We do not run ads. We do not use analytics, advertising cookies, or other tracking. These pages do not set cookies, and they do not load fonts, scripts, or images from another company.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, may record basic connection data such as an IP address, browser type, and the page requested, in its own logs. That processing is described in GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not use those logs to advertise, and we do not build a profile of you from them.

If you email us, we receive the address you wrote from, your message, and anything you attach. We use that only to read what you sent and to reply. We do not add you to a mailing list. We do not run a newsletter.

DNA Forge

DNA Forge does not ask you to create an account, and it does not ask you to sign in. There is no place to enter your real name. The profile you can pick is a drawn head and hat from art in the game. It is not a photo of you.

The game does not show ads. It does not include analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting tools from us or from other companies. It does not send your gameplay to us in the background.

Google Play and the App Store may still collect their own information, such as crash or diagnostics data, when you install and play a game from their stores. That collection is theirs. It is described in the Google Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Settings includes a Privacy Policy row that opens this page in your browser.

Information stored on your device

DNA Forge keeps your progress on the device. That includes animals you have finished, scores, puzzle progress such as locks, sound and music levels, vibration and power-saver settings, a total of how long you have played, Daily Forge progress, and the head and hat you picked. It also stores a yes-or-no note of whether the full game is unlocked.

If you have Android’s backup turned on in your Google account, Android may include this saved progress in the device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your own Google account and the Google Privacy Policy. Valorwright does not receive it or have access to it. You can turn it off in your phone’s backup settings.

That information is written to a save file in the app’s private storage on the device. There is no cloud save. We do not receive a copy, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling DNA Forge, or clearing its storage in your device settings, deletes it.

Bug reports

Settings, and the pause menu during a round, include a Report a Bug button. Using it is optional. The game does not send a report on its own.

If you tap it, DNA Forge opens a Google Form in your browser. The link includes the screen or animal you were on, the game version, and your device model and operating-system version. Google receives those details when the form opens. The form asks what happened, what you expected, how to make it happen again, and how serious it was. You may type an email address if you want a reply. You can close the form without submitting it.

If you submit the form, Google stores your answers and we read them so we can fix the game. We do not sell those reports. Google’s handling of the form is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

DNA Forge offers one optional, one-time purchase that unlocks the rest of the game. On Android, you buy it through Google Play. When DNA Forge is on the App Store, you buy it through Apple. Google and Apple process the payment. We never receive your card number or your full payment details.

The store tells the game whether that purchase is owned. The game saves that yes-or-no on your device. Restore Purchases in Settings asks the store to check again, for example after you reinstall. Google and Apple keep their own records of the purchase under the Google Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

What we share

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers, and we do not share it for advertising.

The sharing this policy describes is GitHub hosting the website, Google hosting a bug report you choose to open or submit, and Google or Apple handling a purchase. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Children

DNA Forge is for ages 13 and up. It is not directed at children under 13. This website is a general studio site, not a site for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game does not ask for a name, an email address, or other contact details. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, for example by email or in a bug report, write to contact@valorwright.com and we will delete it.

How long we keep information

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, removes that local copy. We do not keep a studio copy of gameplay.

Email we receive is kept long enough to correspond with you, then deleted. Bug reports are kept while we need them to find and fix problems, then deleted. You can ask us to delete an email or a bug report sooner.

Your requests

You can ask us what personal information we have about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also tell us not to sell your information. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. Asking will not change your access to the game.

These are the kinds of requests described by the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and similar laws in other places. Write to contact@valorwright.com. Say what you want and how we can reach you. We may need enough detail to find the right email or bug report. We will answer within the time the law that applies to you requires.

Information that never leaves your device is not in our hands. To delete that, uninstall DNA Forge or clear its storage.

Players outside the United States

DNA Forge is released worldwide. Valorwright Games LLC is a company in the United States. If you email us, or submit a bug report, from another country, we process that information in the United States. Google, Apple, and GitHub may process information in the countries where they operate. Their policies describe that.

Changes

We may update this policy when the site or the game changes. When we do, we change the effective date at the top of this page. The current version is always the one published at valorwright.com/privacy.
