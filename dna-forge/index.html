The puzzle

DNA Forge is a breeding puzzle game set in a creature lab. Each round shows a goal animal with four traits. You pick two parents and breed babies, then choose which ones to keep. Every choice moves you closer to the target or further from it, and you have a limited number of turns to land a 100% match.

You start with farm animals and work up through tiers to mythic creatures like dragons, griffins, hydras, phoenixes and cerberus. Each new animal unlocks only after you perfect the one before it. Gene Locks and Gold Locks let you protect a trait when the odds turn against you.

Rare golden trait pieces sometimes show up on a baby. Carry one through to a perfect finish to claim it, then collect every golden piece to build a one-of-a-kind golden animal for your Golden Gallery.
